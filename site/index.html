The 28-day timeline

One foundation. Then your path.

Everyone starts on the same track — two weeks of foundations every builder needs. Then in Weeks 3-4 you branch into the track that fits what you want to make.

Foundations · Shared track Weeks 1-2 · Days 1-14
Fundamentals, prompting & your first build

Everyone starts here, assuming zero experience. You'll learn what AI actually is (and isn't), what today's platforms can really do, and how to prompt with intention — from the fundamentals to advanced techniques. Then it gets hands-on: research and validate an idea, think in MVPs, and ship your first build. You'll write a real spec, learn what to do when AI fails, and meet the builder tools — Cursor, Claude Code, and Codex — with a first look at AI agents and working with data. By Day 14, you pick your path.

Track 1 · Weeks 3-4
Web apps
  • Usually when your idea is something people open, click through, and interact with — a tool, a form, a small product.
  • Or when you want a real interface: pages, buttons, inputs, and a place for users to actually do something.
  • It also makes sense when you're turning a manual process or a spreadsheet into something people can genuinely use.
  • And it's a great fit for problems that sound like: “I wish there were an app that just let me…”
If that sounds like your project, then the web app track might be the right fit for you.

Track 2 · Weeks 3-4
AI agents
  • Usually when your idea involves automation — things like “when this happens, do that.”
  • Or when your users would benefit from AI acting on their behalf.
  • It also makes sense when the core value is saving time on repetitive, multi-step tasks…
  • …or when the problem involves pulling together information from multiple sources.
If that sounds like your project, then the AI agent track might be the right fit for you.
View the AI Agents track →
Track 3 · Weeks 3-4
Data dashboards
  • Usually when your app is about tracking, measuring, or monitoring something.
  • Or when the value comes from helping users spot patterns they'd miss on their own.
  • Especially useful when users make decisions based on numbers — sales, health, finance, operations, or productivity.
  • And a perfect fit for problems that sound like: “I have the data… I just don't know what to do with it.”
If that sounds like your project, then the data dashboard track might be the right path for you.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you start.

Do I need any coding or AI experience?

None at all. Weeks 1 and 2 start from “what AI actually is” and assume zero coding and zero AI experience. If you can write an email, you can start Day 1 — and the nerves fade fast once your hands are on the tools.

How much time does it take each day?

About an hour a day: at least one short video, then a hands-on task to put it into practice. It's self-paced, so a busy day can be a light one — you just pick up where you left off.

Is it included in my Treehouse subscription?

Yes — the bootcamp is part of your Treehouse membership, at no extra cost. Already a member? It's in your library right now. New here? Start with a free trial.

Do I need to pay for AI tools?

You can start everything on free plans. A few of the builder tools you meet in weeks 3–4 (like Claude Code or Cursor) go further on a paid plan — we'll tell you exactly when upgrading is worth it, so you never pay out of confusion.

How do I start?

Hit Start Free Trial (or open it from your library if you're already a member) and press play on Day 1. It tells you exactly what to watch and what to build — no setup guesswork.

Will what I learn be outdated in six months?

The tools will change. The path won't. You go from the fundamentals in weeks 1–2 to shipping a real project in weeks 3–4, learning how to move from chatting to building. That skill transfers to whatever tool ships next.
