The 28-day timeline

One foundation. Then your path.

Everyone starts on the same trunk — two weeks of foundations every builder needs. Then in Weeks 3–4 you branch into the track that fits what you want to make.

Foundations · Shared track Weeks 1–2 · Days 1–14
Chat tools, prompting & your first builds

Everyone starts here. You live in the chat tools — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity — and level up fast: framing, few-shot, chain of thought, spotting mistakes, and safety habits. Then context goes deep with Projects, mega-prompts, and the context-file pattern the pros use. You learn to write a spec, get your first taste of builder tools, and start the prompt library that grows the whole way through.

Track 1 · Weeks 3–4
Web apps
  • Usually when your idea is something people open, click through, and interact with — a tool, a form, a small product.
  • Or when you want a real interface: pages, buttons, inputs, and a place for users to actually do something.
  • It also makes sense when you're turning a manual process or a spreadsheet into something people can genuinely use.
  • And it's a great fit for problems that sound like: “I wish there were an app that just let me…”
If that sounds like your project, then the web app track might be the right fit for you.
Track 2 · Weeks 3–4
AI agents
  • Usually when your idea involves automation — things like “when this happens, do that.”
  • Or when your users would benefit from AI acting on their behalf.
  • It also makes sense when the core value is saving time on repetitive, multi-step tasks…
  • …or when the problem involves pulling together information from multiple sources.
If that sounds like your project, then the AI agent track might be the right fit for you.
Track 3 · Weeks 3–4
Data dashboards
  • Usually when your app is about tracking, measuring, or monitoring something.
  • Or when the value comes from helping users spot patterns they'd miss on their own.
  • Especially useful when users make decisions based on numbers — sales, health, finance, operations, or productivity.
  • And a perfect fit for problems that sound like: “I have the data… I just don't know what to do with it.”
If that sounds like your project, then the data dashboard track might be the right path for you.

The questions you're already asking.

“Honestly, AI makes me nervous.”

That's more common than you think, and it's a fair response to all the noise. We move one small step at a time, and you build safety habits from Day 1: what to trust, what to double-check, what to keep private. The nervousness fades fast once your hands are on the tools.

“I'm not technical at all.”

Neither were most of our students. Day 1 assumes zero coding and zero AI experience. If you can write an email, you can start this bootcamp.

“I don't have time.”

Plan on about an hour a day: 15–20 minutes of video, the rest hands-on practice. And it's fully self-paced. Busy week? Do the floor. Free weekend? Chase the ceiling.

“Do I need to pay for AI tools?”

No. Free plans carry you through the bootcamp. We also teach you exactly when upgrading is worth it, so you never pay out of confusion.

“Won't this be outdated in six months?”

The tools might update. The path won't. You're learning how to move from chatting to teaching to building. That skill transfers to every tool that ships next year, and the year after.

“What if I fall behind?”

The 5+2 rhythm has breathing room built in, and the community keeps you moving. Falling behind for a few days is normal. Staying on the sideline for another year is the actual risk.
